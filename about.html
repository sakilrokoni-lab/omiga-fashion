The house

Built around the buyer's order.

OMIGA Fashion opened in Dhaka in 2016. The brief has stayed narrow on purpose: men's, ladies', and kids' wear, made with factories the team already knows.

How the house works

Specialists, not a general trader.

A narrow product focus gives the merchandising team a sharper eye. Customers receive designs across mid-to-high quality fashion, priced through direct factory relationships rather than a long chain of agents.

Over the years the house has pushed partner factories toward new products, stronger technical knowledge, and higher compliance. More than 30 reputed factories sit in the active network, and the current roster of partners is above 50. Quality and compliance staff run regular internal audits. A full factory profile can be shared for any facility a buyer wants to review.

Md. Badiuz Zaman is owner and CEO, working from the Uttara office in Dhaka.
