About us

A buying house with a difference.

OMIGA Fashion is an apparel sourcing and manufacturing company in Dhaka, with a reputation built on quality, reliability, and customer service.

We focus only on men's, ladies', and kids' wear. That narrow brief gives the team a sharper eye for fabric, fit, and trims, and lets buyers get mid-to-high quality fashion at a competitive price.

You send the tech pack, reference, or target price. We place it with the right factory from our partner network and follow it through every sample, inspection, and shipment point.

Phase 01 · Development

Brief to approved price and plan.

  1. SamplingWe collect your fabric and style requirements, pick the right source, screen the first make with inspection and testing, then send samples and bring your comments back to the factory.
  2. SourcingLocal fabric and accessories from Bangladesh. Imported fabric and accessories from China and Hong Kong.
  3. PricingSupplier quotations compared against each supplier's quality record and capacity to meet the contract, then negotiated and finalised.
  4. Order planningOnce the order is confirmed, we plan the process with the supplier and build a critical path that highlights the stages that can slip.

Phase 02 · Production to shipment

Bulk to on-time delivery.

  1. Sample trajectoryPrototype, salesman, pre-production, and photo samples are tracked as separate milestones.
  2. Quality assuranceBasic parameters tested on every dye lot. Fabric, size-set, inline, and final inspections arranged and carried out.
  3. Weekly progress reportMerchandising, quality control, and logistics meet every Saturday. Status is emailed to you every Monday.
  4. Final inspectionPerformed to AQL, or 100%, based on your requirement.
  5. ShipmentVessel arranged with the forwarder, and shipment points followed up for smooth connections.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp or WeChat.

Chat on WhatsApp
What products do you source?

Men's, ladies', and kids' wear across woven tops, woven bottoms, knit tops, knit bottoms, and sweaters. Browse the gallery to see styles we have already developed.

Where do fabrics and accessories come from?

Local fabric and accessories from Bangladesh, and imported fabric and accessories from China and Hong Kong when a style needs them.

How is quality checked?

We test basic parameters on every dye lot, then arrange fabric, size-set, inline, and final inspections. The final inspection follows AQL or 100% checking, based on your requirement.

How will I know where my order stands?

Our merchandising, quality control, and logistics staff meet every Saturday, and we email you a status on every Monday.

Which samples do you follow?

Prototype, salesman, pre-production, and photo samples, each followed up as its own step.

Can I see a factory profile before I order?

Yes. We can share individual factory profiles on request, including the certifications each facility holds.

Do you handle shipping?

Yes. We arrange the vessel with your forwarder and follow up each shipment point for a smooth connection.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the style, fabric, and factory. Send your quantity with the enquiry and we will confirm what each factory can run.

How do I start?

Send a tech pack, a reference photo, or a target price by email, WhatsApp, or WeChat. We reply with a factory fit, a sample route, and a costing.
