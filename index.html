About the house

A buying house with a difference.

OMIGA Fashion is an apparel sourcing company in Bangladesh. The work stays inside men's, ladies', and kids' wear, so the team reads a style the way a factory needs it read: fabric, fit, trim, and the date it has to sail.

Buyers get mid-to-high quality fashion at a workable price, because the factory relationships are direct and the follow-up is weekly.

Our continued success has evolved from the success of our customers.

What we do

From first sketch
to the vessel.

01

Sourcing & merchandising

Fabrics and trims from trusted factories in Bangladesh, with imports from China and Hong Kong when the style needs them.

02

Pattern & cutting

Pattern making and cutting that stay consistent from the salesman sample through the bulk cut.

03

Quality assurance

Fabric tests, size-set, inline checks, and a final inspection to AQL or 100%, as the buyer requires.

04

Technical support

Design interpretation, fit sessions, and spec development with a team that stays on the style.

05

Production oversight

A critical path from fabric in-house to finishing, packing, and shipment readiness.

06

Collection design

Curated ranges for men, ladies, and kids when a buyer wants a programme, not a single style.
